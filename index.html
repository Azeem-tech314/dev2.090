What is the difference between breakfast and brunch?
Breakfast runs 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM and focuses on eggs, pancakes, bacon, coffee. Brunch runs 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, primarily on weekends, and blends breakfast items with lunch dishes plus cocktails like mimosas and Bloody Marys. Brunch pricing typically runs 30 to 60 percent higher than breakfast at the same restaurant.
Where can I find 24-hour breakfast places?
Waffle House operates 1,509 verified locations open 24 hours across the Southeast and Midwest. Denny's has 24-hour service at approximately 40 percent of its 822 stores. Jack in the Box (1,852) runs 24/7 at most locations. Casey's General Store (1,115) is 24-hour with breakfast pizza and donuts. Local 24-hour diners like Peppermill Restaurant in Las Vegas and Cafe du Monde in New Orleans are also on our map. Use the 24-Hour filter above.
Do McDonald's, Chick-fil-A, or Wendy's serve breakfast on weekends?
Yes. McDonald's extends breakfast to 11:00 AM on weekends versus 10:30 AM on weekdays at most US locations. Chick-fil-A serves breakfast Monday through Saturday until 10:30 AM but is closed all Sunday at every location. Wendy's serves breakfast every day of the week until 10:30 AM though hours vary by franchise.

Starbucks (9,893) is second for breakfast reach with all-day service. Dunkin operates 6,510 verified US stores. Waffle House operates 1,509 locations, every one open 24 hours.
