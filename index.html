Frequently Asked

What are the best breakfast places near me?

Use the search box or "Use My Location" button on the map above. It sorts by distance, rating or price, and filters by 20 status tags including Serving Now, All‑Day, Under $10, Halal, Vegan and Wheelchair‑accessible. Neighborhood picks appear once you zoom into a city.

What time do breakfast places open?

Most US fast‑food breakfast starts 5–6 AM. Sit‑down chains (IHOP, Denny's, Waffle House) run 24 hours. Independent diners typically open 6–7 AM weekdays and 7–8 AM weekends. See the live chain table above for exact times in your timezone.

Which breakfast chains serve breakfast all day?

Sonic, Jack in the Box, IHOP, Denny's, Waffle House, and many Bojangles locations. Also Cracker Barrel until 11 AM then lunch begins. See the "All‑Day" filter on the map for a full list.

What is the cheapest fast‑food breakfast?

Wendy's Sausage/Bacon Biscuit ($1.99), Taco Bell's Cheesy Toasted Breakfast Burrito ($1.99), and Burger King's Croissan'wich ($2.49) are the lowest tickets. See the "Cheap under $5" table above.

What breakfast places are open 24 hours?

Waffle House, IHOP (most), Denny's (most), Jack in the Box, and select Whataburger locations. Filter "24‑Hour" on the map.

Which chains still serve breakfast after 10:30 AM?

Taco Bell (11:00 AM), Whataburger (11:00 AM), Cracker Barrel (11:00 AM), plus all all‑day chains. See late‑risers table above.

Do you cover independent diners or only chains?

Both. This build tracks 30 major chains (200 sample pins) and 500 curated independent breakfast spots across 60 US cities.

How is data verified?

Chain hours are pulled from the brands' published breakfast policies. Independent spots are cross‑checked against Google Business, Yelp and city tourism boards. Restaurants change addresses and hours — call before a long drive.

Can I add my favorite spot to Favorites?

Yes — tap the ❤️ in any pin popup. Favorites save to your device (localStorage) and can be filtered via the ❤️ Favorites chip.

Does the map work offline?

Pins load from a static JSON embedded in this page (no API required after first load). Base map tiles need internet. Search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim (free).
